# Barents Sea food web: threats and ecosystem services

Interactive visualization of the Barents Sea food web, showing which species are
directly affected by eight anthropogenic threats and which species provide four
ecosystem services, for the metaweb and each of the 25 subregions.

**Open the visualization:** https://annaeklof.github.io/visualization_barents_sea_foodweb/

It accompanies the manuscript *Cascading effects of species loss on ecosystem
service provisioning in a marine food web* by Anna Eklöf, Johanna Sollén Mattsson
and György Barabás (in review).

## Using the visualization

- Species are placed vertically by trophic level. Hover over a species to see
  its name and highlight its links (one or two steps away, set under *Hover*).
- *Subregion* switches between the metaweb and the 25 subregions. A subregion
  can also be linked to directly, e.g. `…/visualization_barents_sea_foodweb/?web=sub21`.
- *Threats* and *Services* show or hide the links from threats to the species
  they affect and from species to the services they provide.
- *Colour* and *Size* choose what node colour and size represent.
- *Export mode* gives a clean view for screenshots.

The page is a single self-contained file (`index.html`); it can also be
downloaded and opened locally in a web browser.

## Data

- **Food webs:** the Barents Sea metaweb and subregional food webs from
  Planque et al. (2014) and Kortsch et al. (2018).
- **Species–threat and species–ecosystem service links:** compiled by the authors
  from the scientific and grey literature, as described in the manuscript.
